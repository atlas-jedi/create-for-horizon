# What is turned off on Horizon

This fork builds Create for Sunrise against the **Horizon** mod SDK (Nightly `f1bb66f`, Minecraft 26.3). Most of the
Sunrise API has an equivalent on Horizon and was adapted. What has none is turned off and listed below: what the player
loses, the Sunrise API Horizon lacks, and where it is. Code that is off is marked `#[cfg(any())] // Horizon: API missing`
or with a `Horizon:` comment. The adaptation leaves the engine (`src/engine`) as it is, but for the two crusher helpers
the crushing wheels' row names.

| (adapted, not cut) Player on a belt: carried by its own client, as in Create, with Create's rule for who rides: the player becomes a passenger of every belt segment its box is in (`BeltBlock.entityInside`) or that holds it up, the segment under its feet wins, and the belt goes on carrying it for one tick after it last touched the belt (three on a slope), so it is carried off the end of a belt and over the edge. But each tick the belt adds a push to the player's velocity (`ClientHost::push_player`, as the fan does) instead of moving the player by the belt's motion (`move(SELF, ...)`), which Horizon's client cannot do. The push is worked out against ground friction (air drag in the air), so within a few ticks it carries the player at the belt's speed, and the player's walking adds on top. Because the belt's motion goes through the velocity and the player's own move: **the camera bobs and the legs walk while the player stands still on a running belt** (Create moves the player after its tick, without velocity, so neither the view bob nor the walk animation sees it); speed builds up over about a quarter of a second; after the belt lets go the player keeps its speed for a few ticks; a slow belt blocked by an entity stops pushing but does not stop the player dead. The server did not send the push, so on a LAN or dedicated server Horizon's movement check logs it (and sends the player back if the server is set to). Slopes use the same contacts but keep the approximate push (no split moves, no lift past the top). ## Adaptations that change behaviour

These are not cuts, but they may behave differently in game.

- Contraptions, stabilized bearings (`StabilizedContraption`, `StabilizedBearingMovementBehaviour`): as in Create, a mechanical bearing at rest on a turning mechanical or windmill bearing's structure (on its axis), on a clock's hand, or anywhere on a cart's structure keeps what it faces as its own structure: it hangs where its front is carried to, upright on a bearing or clock; on a cart a vertical bearing's never turns and a horizontal one's follows the cart's yaw but not its pitch (`updateOrientation`). It has its own collider, storage and block data (its own key, `contraption::sub_key`), its actors work where it hangs (a stall stalls its carrier), its contraption controls on a bearing's structure can be clicked while it rides, it is saved with its carrier, and it goes back where its front went, unturned (on a cart, by its own yaw's quarter) (`src/glue/contraptions/bearings.rs`, `lifts.rs`, `draw.rs`, `colliders.rs`, `src/glue/trains/carts.rs`, `visuals.rs`). A mechanical bearing's top turns back against its carrier wherever Create's `canBeStabilized` allows, with or without a structure in front (`StabilizedBearingMovementBehaviour`): held still on an on-axis bearing of a turning bearing's or clock hand's structure, and on a cart turned back by the cart's yaw (vertical) or pitch (horizontal, along the cart's initial orientation). Carts and train carriages now show their kinetic blocks' parts (`renderBlockEntities`: shaft halves, cogs, bearing tops and the like, at rest) as displays beside their blocks; a carried mechanical bearing shows its top only. Contraption controls can be clicked on a turning bearing's structure, on a clock's hands, on a cart's or train carriage's structure, and on what rides any of them. A cart's and a carriage's blocks are block displays the server poses, so each tick the server sends the players within 16 blocks of their controls each structure's controls and pose (`draw::POSED_CHANNEL`, `actors::PosedControls`) and the client finds them with its ray from that: the pose is the server's last tick's, not the frame's, so on a fast cart or train the ray can be off by up to a tick's travel, and their button shows no press (those controls are block displays, without Create's renderer). A carriage carries its controls and the kinds they turned off, saved with its train, and its actors start with those kinds off (`ContraptionControlsMovement`); its sliding doors are actors the controls turn off too, a door's kind on all the train's carriages (`ContraptionControlsMovingInteraction`), and a door turned off stays shut at stations. A clock's hands work as Create's two contraptions: each hand's actors work where it has turned to, an actor's stall holds its hand only (`isStalled`), and each hand, like each structure riding one, has its own storage, glue, controls and kinds turned off. Running mechanical bearings, clockwork bearings and rope pulleys refuse to be moved, as in Create (`BlockMovementChecksImpl.isMovementAllowedFallback`); a running windmill bearing is carried, stops and starts again on its own (`disassembleForMovement`, `QueueAssembly`).
- Moving rope, magnets and rope pulleys: Create's `onRemove` skips rope a structure moves (`isMoving`). Horizon's `block_changed` has no flags, so the glue counts its own writes with `UPDATE_MOVE_BY_PISTON` (`server::moving_write`, `src/glue/server.rs`) and the rope it moves itself is not collapsed (`lifts::collapse_ropes`). Collapses run in the level's tick after the change, not inside the write.
- Mirrored rope pulleys (`startMirroringOther`): a rope pulley over the top row of another pulley's structure follows it as in Create, but one that is running a structure of its own, or already mirrors another, is left alone (Create would let it mirror and freeze its own structure). Which pulleys mirror which is saved with the pulley they follow (`mirrors`), not with each mirroring pulley (`MirrorParent`) (`src/glue/contraptions/lifts.rs`).
- Sticker facing up: walking on it slows as on slime through the block's speed factor (0.4), so a sneaking entity or one moving up or down fast is slowed too (Create's `stepOn` spares them: `0.4 + |dy| x 0.2` while `|dy| < 0.1`, not stepping carefully). Its attach and detach sounds and the attach slime come from the server a tick after its state changes, timed to Create's head chase (four ticks to take hold, one to let go), where Create's client plays them as it draws the head (`src/glue/contraptions/actors.rs`, `tick_stickers`). Its bounce is Create's (`bounce_restitution`), and its fall still hurts.
- Mechanical piston: the slime from a slime ball put on it is sent by the server (Create spawns it on the client) (`src/glue/contraptions/bearings.rs`, `PistonPart`).
- Super glue: removing an area with an attack goes from the client (`InteractionKey::Attack`, in the air or on a block) to the server on `create:glue_remove`, and the server takes off the area the player's look picks, of those near them; Create's packet names the glue entity the client picked (`SuperGlueRemovalPacket`). A left click on a block is also taken on the server (`left_click_block`) (`src/glue/contraptions/superglue.rs`).

- Weighted Ejector: observers watching it still pulse when it fires (`launchItems` sends them a shape update, and `ObserverBlock.startSignal` schedules their tick in 2), but the mod API cannot send that update or schedule a tick from a block entity, so the glue runs each watching observer's tick (`LevelRef::block_tick`) 2 ticks later itself, if it is still unpowered (`src/glue/logistics/arms.rs`, `watching_observers_see`).
- Depots: the landing sound of a stack a hopper or another mod puts into an empty depot through its item handler (`DEPOT_PLOP`) plays at the end of the tick, once it is clear the transaction kept the stack (a handler cannot learn its transaction aborted) (`src/glue/logistics/depot.rs`, `landed_later`).
- Content declarations live in `data/create/horizon/` and `assets/create/horizon/`, where Horizon reads them (Sunrise reads `.../sunrise/`).
- `state_for_placement` and `can_survive` get only a `&dyn LevelHost` on Horizon, without the side. The code treats them as server side (`src/glue/compat.rs`, `lifts.rs`, `redstone/parts.rs`, `copycat/parts.rs`). On the client this may queue work only the server consumes.
- Horizon's `block_changed` does not give the change's flags; kinetics always gets an ordinary update (no `KNOWN_SHAPE`). `src/lib.rs`.
- `block_entity_client_tick` goes through an adapter (`compat::ClientTick`) that builds the block entity's view from Horizon's event.
- Item capabilities (depot, belt, funnel, millstone, mixer and basin, crafter, toolbox) go through an adapter (`compat::be_offer`) from Sunrise's "offer" shape to Horizon's "return the handler" shape.
- `ResourceHandler`: `insert_at`/`extract_at` became `insert`/`extract`; Sunrise's index-less `insert`/`extract` became `insert_any`/`extract_any`; `capacity` always gets the resource (no longer an `Option`).
- Particles: `register_particle_type` with codecs became `particle_key` plus a provider registered by name (`compat::ParticleType`).
- Create's debug commands: run through the global `run_mod_command` hook instead of a closure per command.
- Stepping on belts and ejectors: from `BlockPart::step_on` to the global `block_step_on` hook (server only).
- Gearshift and clutch: `state_for_placement` gets a level that reads no redstone, so the power `getStateForPlacement` gives is read in `on_block_placed_by`, right after the item places the block and before any tick (`src/glue/logistics/placement.rs`, `ShiftItem`).
- Mechanical Press: plays its activation sound as the vanilla sounds Create's `sounds.json` names (anvil land or wool hit, then item break), so it needs nothing from the jar.
- Engineer's Goggles: on the head they use the 3D `create:block/goggles` model, as Create's `GogglesModel` does.
- Press crumbs, saw crumbs, deployer crumbs at the hand, the blaze burner's flame burst when fed and a haunted horse's soul flames are sent by the server as particles (Create spawns them on each client from its own packet or tick), so they reach the players within the server's particle range, and the burst is seen by every nearby player, not only the one feeding (`src/glue/processing/press.rs`, `saw.rs`, `deployer.rs`, `basin.rs`, `fan.rs`).
- Millstone and crushing wheel sounds: Create's `SoundScapes` (machines in one pitch band share one looping sound at their average position) run on the client from what the server tells of each machine (`Milling`, `Crushing`), in `src/glue/processing/ambient.rs`.
- Belt connector: with a first shaft remembered, its client draws the line toward the shaft looked at, green dust when the two connect and red when not (`BeltConnectorHandler`; the Sunrise port did not have it). Belts at 45° connect as in Create.
- Sliding doors (andesite, brass, copper, train, framed glass): a content block has one `render_shape` for all its states, where Create's `SlidingDoorBlock.getRenderShape` stops drawing a door's model while it is not `visible`. The committed blockstates (`assets/create/blockstates/*_door.json`, written by `create-data compile`) have cases for the `visible` states only, so a hidden door draws nothing of its own and its block entity draws the panel, as in Create.
- Sliding doors placed by a player: the hinge is `DoorBlock.getHinge`'s, worked out by the glue once the door is placed (`on_block_placed_by`, inside the placement, before a tick sees it; the placing client's own prediction can show the template's hinge until the server's update arrives) from the clicked point and what stands on each side (a lower half of the same door, full collision cubes from `collision_boxes`), and both halves take it. The iron door template's own hinge did not follow that rule for Create's doors in game (two train doors in a two-wide doorway between full walls both hinged right, so they never made the double door `isDoubleDoor` needs), and `state_for_placement` gets no level to read collision shapes from (`src/glue/blocks.rs`, `DoorPlacement`; `src/engine/src/door.rs`, `placement_hinge`).
- Sliding doors opened by redstone: the iron door template's `neighborChanged` opens the door without hiding it. Both halves are hidden inside that same write by `update_shape` (`door_half_follows`, `src/glue/blocks.rs`) instead of by Create's `neighborChanged`, and the other door of a double door follows after the level's tick, in the same tick (`tick_doors`). A door placed next to a powered block is placed open by the template, where Create places it closed and opens it a tick later, with the sound and the slide.
- Train arrival chimes (`ArrivalSoundQueue`: a carriage's bells, note blocks and steam whistles as its train ends a journey of more than 100 blocks): each plays where its carriage is as it starts, where Create binds it to the carriage entity (`playSound(null, entity, ...)`) and it moves with it; a carriage is no entity here (`src/glue/trains/arrival.rs`).
- Train status (`TrainStatus`): "Track missing" is not told when the track a train stood on leaves with an unloaded chunk (Create keeps its track graph whole; here a column's track unloads with it), only when it is taken away; and there is no "double portal" message, as portal tracks are left out (`src/engine/src/trains.rs`, `ensure`).
- Train conductors: a carriage keeps the conductors it had while it stands where entities don't tick or its riders saved with it are not seated again yet, as Create keeps them while its carriage entity is unloaded (`src/glue/trains/conductor.rs`, `update`). The client tells a player sitting on a carriage seat (the steam puffs are a little quieter, `seatCrossfade`) by their riding an item display, the seats' stand-in (`src/glue/trains/effects.rs`).
- Sliding doors on train carriages and carts are block displays: an opening door's halves glide evenly to where Create's renderer puts them, over the panel's 7 ticks (display interpolation), where Create's panel moves by the square of how far it has got (slow at first, fast at the end). A folding door's leaves glide straight from shut to folded instead of swinging on their hinges (`src/glue/trains/visuals.rs`, `slide_door`). No door riding a structure (carriage, cart, elevator) plays the iron door's closing sound at 1/8 volume on each client when its panel comes to rest (`SlidingDoorMovementBehaviour.tick`).
- Station, signal and train observer picks (`TrackTargetingBlockItem`): the graph is the server's, so the preview of a pick on the track looked at (`TrackTargetingClient`) is asked of the server as the hover changes, and the marker shows once it answers (Create works it out on the client from its own copy of the graph). A station, signal or observer with no pick in its block entity (placed by a command, or saved before the port kept picks) binds to the straight track beside it, as the port did before (`src/glue/trains/tracks.rs`, `src/engine/src/network.rs`, `beside`).
- Signal sections shown while a signal is held (`tickSignalOverlay`, `TrackGraphVisualizer.visualiseSignalEdgeGroups`): the graph is the server's, so the server works out the coloured lines around each player holding a signal every 5 ticks and sends them when they change, and the client draws each as Catnip's 1/8-block beam (`Outliner.showLine`), its sides lit as entities are. A section's colour (`SignalEdgeGroup.color`) is not kept from when its signal was placed: each section takes the first colour none of the sections across its signals has, the boundaries taken in the graph's order, so sections side by side still differ but may not be the colours Create would have shown. Crossings don't share their sections' colours (`intersecting`), since the port's graph keeps no crossing groups (`src/engine/src/network.rs`, `signal_overlay`; `src/glue/trains/track_client.rs`).
